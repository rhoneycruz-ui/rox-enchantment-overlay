# Technical Glow

Technical Glow treats darkness as working space rather than emptiness. Deep navy fields hold precise frames, measured interruptions and quiet traces of machine vision. Space is disciplined and generous: every bright mark earns its position, and every interval feels calibrated by a master craftsperson rather than filled by habit.

Color behaves like an instrument panel seen at night. Electric cyan carries observation, restrained chartreuse signals a verified result, and soft mineral whites establish structure without glare. Their balance must feel painstakingly tuned, with the polish and restraint of work refined through countless careful passes.

Form comes from detection rather than decoration: scanning brackets, bounding boxes, progress segments, crystalline facets and aligned points. Repetition creates rhythm, while one luminous focal structure anchors the field. The visual system should suggest intelligence through spatial relationships, never through literal characters, borrowed game imagery or explanatory prose.

Scale moves between a few decisive geometric gestures and fine diagnostic marks. Large forms establish confidence; small reference lines reward close inspection. The composition must remain calm under this density, showing the master-level control of someone who has labored over every alignment, radius and optical balance.

Typography, when present, is sparse and clinical, but the preferred expression is almost wordless. Craft is visible in edge quality, chromatic restraint and exact negative space. The finished work should feel meticulously built by a top practitioner: an original technical artifact whose sophistication is communicated visually before it is understood intellectually.
